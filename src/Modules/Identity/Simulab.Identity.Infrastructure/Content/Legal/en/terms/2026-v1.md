## 1. What Simulab is

Simulab is a study service. It runs practice exams and tells you what to study next. Creating an
account means accepting these terms.

## 2. Your account

You must be 18 or older. Give a real email address, keep your password to yourself, and tell us if
someone else uses your account.

## 3. Content

Past exams belong to the organisations that wrote them and are shown here for study only. Practice
results are estimates, never a promise of a result in a real exam.

## 4. Ending the agreement

You can ask for your account to be erased at any time. We can close an account that breaks these
terms, and we say why when we do.
