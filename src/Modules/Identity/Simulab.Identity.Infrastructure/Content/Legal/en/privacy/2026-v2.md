## 1. What we collect

Your email address, the name you choose to give, what you answer in practice sessions, and the
consent you gave when you signed up.

## 2. Why we keep it

To run your practice sessions, to show your progress, and to tell you what to study next. We do not
sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

## 3. How long

While your account exists. When an account is erased we remove what identifies you and keep the
answers without a name, so question statistics stay correct.

## 4. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or erase your data, and to withdraw your consent.

## 5. Where your data is stored

For every user, in every country, your data is stored with Microsoft Azure in the Brazil South
region (São Paulo): the database, the session cache and the file storage.

## 6. Who processes your data for us

These companies process personal data on our behalf:

| Company | What it does for us | Data it receives | Where it processes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Microsoft Azure | Hosting, database, session cache and file storage | All the data described in section 1 | Brazil South (São Paulo) |
| Anthropic | The Claude API behind the study coach | The content of your practice sessions | United States |
| SendGrid (Twilio) | Sending emails: sign-up verification, password reset and account farewell | Your email address, your name and the content of the email | United States |

## 7. If you are in the European Union

Storing your data in Brazil rests on Article 45 of the GDPR: the European Commission recognised
Brazil as giving adequate protection (Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/179 of 26 January 2026).
Sending data to the United States rests on safeguards that each company states in its own terms:

- Anthropic: the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission (Decision (EU) 2021/914).
- SendGrid (Twilio): Twilio Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; where it does
  not apply, the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

## 8. If you are in Brazil

Storing your data in Brazil is not an international transfer. Sending data to the United States
rests on Article 33 of the LGPD:

- Anthropic: the standard contractual clauses approved by the ANPD (Resolution CD/ANPD 19/2024).
- SendGrid (Twilio): the standard contractual clauses approved by the ANPD (Resolution CD/ANPD 19/2024).

## 9. When this list changes

When we change a company in this list, we publish a new version of this policy.
