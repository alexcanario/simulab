## 1. What we collect

Your email address, the name you choose to give, what you answer in practice sessions, and the
consent you gave when you signed up.

## 2. Why we keep it

To run your practice sessions, to show your progress, and to tell you what to study next. We do not
sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

## 3. How long

While your account exists. When an account is erased we remove what identifies you and keep the
answers without a name, so question statistics stay correct.

## 4. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or erase your data, and to withdraw your consent.

## 5. Where your data is stored

For every user, in every country, your data is stored with Microsoft Azure in the Central US region
(Iowa, United States): the database, the session cache and the file storage. The only exception is
email: the service that sends our emails stores its data in Brazil.

## 6. Who processes your data for us

These companies process personal data on our behalf:

| Company | What it does for us | Data it receives | Where it processes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Microsoft Azure | Hosting, database, session cache and file storage | All the data described in section 1 | Central US (United States) |
| Microsoft Azure Communication Services | Sending emails: sign-up verification, password reset, password changed and account farewell | Your email address, your name and the content of the email | Data location Brazil |
| Anthropic | The Claude API behind the study coach, when the study coach is on. Today no data of yours is sent to it | The content of your practice sessions, once the study coach is on | United States |

## 7. If you are in the European Union

Storing your data in the United States rests on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Article 45 of the
GDPR) when the receiving company is certified for that data, and on the standard contractual clauses
approved by the European Commission (Decision (EU) 2021/914, Article 46 of the GDPR) when it is not.

- Microsoft: it states that it complies with the Data Privacy Framework for the U.S. companies that
  use its brand, and it offers the standard contractual clauses in its data protection addendum. We
  have not yet confirmed that the certification covers the hosting service, so we do not rely on it alone.
- Anthropic: the standard contractual clauses of its data processing addendum. They matter once the
  study coach sends data to it.
- Emails stored in Brazil rest on Article 45 of the GDPR: the European Commission recognised Brazil as
  giving adequate protection (Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/179 of 26 January 2026).

## 8. If you are in Brazil

Storing your data in the United States is an international transfer under Article 33 of the LGPD. The
mechanism is the standard contractual clauses approved by the ANPD (Resolution CD/ANPD 19/2024). They
are being put in place with Microsoft, and will be put in place with Anthropic before the study coach
sends it any data. Emails stored in Brazil involve no transfer of data at rest.

Until these safeguards are in place, the service holds test accounts only.

## 9. When this list changes

When we change a company in this list, we publish a new version of this policy. This version
replaces version 2026-v2, which named another region and another email company.
