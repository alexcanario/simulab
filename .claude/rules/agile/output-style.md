# Output style

- Talk to the owner in Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR): answers, questions, reports and summaries of what a subagent found. Only what is written to the repository is English.
- What you write to the board for the owner (issue title, body, comments, PR of `Merge: pr`) is pt-BR, rendered whole from the English file: `references/board.md`, "Language of the board".
- Keep code, identifiers, paths, commands and quoted tool output as they are; never translate them.
- A technical term (severity, service, acronym, protocol, library) goes to the owner, in chat and documents, with its pt-BR word from "Technical terms" in `docs/glossary.md`; the skill that writes a document adds the row the first time one is used.
- Answer first. The first sentence is the answer, the result or the question — never a preamble, a recap of the request or praise.
- A step report is at most 10 lines: what changed (files), result (real numbers), what is next, who acts next.
- Details live in the file, not in the chat. Link the file; never paste its content unless the owner asks.
- Be long only when: a gate failed (paste the real error), a question needs context, or the owner asks for the explanation.
- One recommendation, with a one-line reason. List alternatives only for a real choice, at most three, each with its trade-off in one line.
- Questions come last, set apart from the report: a question card, otherwise a quoted `❓` block (`conventions.md`, "Questions to the owner"). A report never goes inside a block.
- Every question, doubt or challenge carries a recommendation and its reason; another pick is scored against it as `conventions.md` ("Questions to the owner") sets.
- A command the owner is to run or copy goes in two code blocks, `powershell` then `bash` (`conventions.md`, "Commands for the owner").
- Do not narrate the work ("now I will...", "let me check..."). Report outcomes, not activity.
- Do not repeat what an earlier message already said. Reference it ("as in F-3, BR2").
- Say "I don't know" or "not verified" in those words. Never fill a gap with a plausible guess.
- Bad news goes first and plain: what failed, what it blocks, what you propose.
- Tables only for data with two or more dimensions; otherwise a short list. No headers in answers under 10 lines.
- Documents follow `references/document-style.md`.
- A subagent returns only: verdict, findings (file:line, one line each), and what it did not check. No narrative.
