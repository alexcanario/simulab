# Acceptance history on the account page

Technical terms: [glossary](../glossary.md)

## Summary
Show people, on their account page, what they accepted and when: each terms and privacy version, the date and the language. Today the data export (F-16) already lists every acceptance, but only as a download; the account page shows nothing. Raised as out of scope by the F-85 refinement, 2026-10-21, which adds one acceptance row per new version and so makes the history worth showing.
