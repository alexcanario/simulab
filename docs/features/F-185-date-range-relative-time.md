## Summary
The kit gains `AppDateRangeField` (presets 7, 30, 90 days and this year, plus a custom range), `AppRelativeTime` ("3 days ago", exact date in the tooltip) and `AppDateText`, in the gallery. The period filters of `AccountEvents` and `RoleHistory`, a select of days today, move to the range field; the audit lists show the relative time.
