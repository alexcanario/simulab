# Annul a question

Technical terms: [glossary](../glossary.md)

## Summary
A curator annuls a published question with a reason, as the organizer did (`AnnulledQuestion`). The question stays in the bank and in attempt history; how an annulled question is scored is the edition's scoring rule (epic E-6), and practice leaves it out. Source: epic Question bank (E-4), brief capability 3.
