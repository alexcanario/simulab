# Accept updated documents

> Frozen on 2026-10-21 (v1.10.0). This page may be out of date; see the [pt-BR version](../pt-BR/accept-new-documents.md).

When the Terms of Use or the Privacy Policy change, Simulab asks you to read what changed and accept it before you continue.

## Who can use it
Anyone who is signed in. The page appears by itself, only when there is something new to accept.

## How to do it
### Accept the new documents
1. Sign in to Simulab as usual. If there are new documents, you land on the **Our documents changed** page.
2. For each document in the list, click **Read** to open the text. Come back to the page when you are done.
3. Click **Accept and continue**. You go on to the page you were opening (or to the home page).

If an even newer version comes out while you read, the page tells you and shows the texts again: read and accept once more.

### If you do not want to accept
There is no decline button. You can:
- click **Sign out** to end the session (the page appears again the next time you sign in);
- open **Account** (the link at the end of the page) to **download your data** or **erase your account**.

Until you accept, you can only open this page, the texts of the documents and the **Account** page.

## What is recorded
Each acceptance keeps the versions, the language, the day and time, and the address you accepted from. Earlier acceptances are not changed and appear in **Download my data**.
