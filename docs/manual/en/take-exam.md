# Take a practice exam

> Frozen on 2026-10-27 (v1.26.0). This page may be out of date; see the [pt-BR version](../pt-BR/take-exam.md).

The simulator is where you take a real past exam, with the same questions in the same order as the paper. You answer one question at a time, follow what you have answered on the answer sheet and submit the exam when you are done.

## Who can use it
Students with the **Take exams** permission. Only you see your practice exam: nobody else opens it, not even an Administrator.

## How to do it
### Start or continue a practice exam
1. Choose **Catalog** in the **Study** section of the menu and open the exam (see [Catalog](catalog.md)).
2. On the row of the published edition, choose **Take this practice exam**.
3. The simulator opens without the app menu. The top bar shows the edition, the **Leave and continue later** link and the theme and language switches.

If you already have a practice exam in progress for that edition, the same button resumes it: it opens on the first blank question, with the answers you already gave.

### Answer
1. Read the question. When it has a supporting text, it appears above the statement, in the **Base text** box.
2. Mark your answer:
   - **Single choice:** mark one option. Click it again to clear.
   - **True or false:** judge the statement. Click your choice again to clear.
   - **Multiple answers:** mark every correct option. The simulator does not say how many there are, because that is part of the key. Unmark them all to clear the answer.
3. The answer is saved as soon as you mark it; there is no confirm button. A line under the question says **Answer saved** or **Answer cleared**.
4. Use **Previous** and **Next** to move through the exam, in paper order.

### Use the answer sheet
1. The answer sheet sits beside the question. On a phone, open it with the **Answer sheet** button, which shows how many you have answered.
2. The numbers are grouped by section, each with its range. The sections are the notice subjects, in the order they appear in the paper; the questions with no subject go to a last section, **Other**.
3. Each number shows whether it is **Answered**, **Blank** or the **Current** one. Choose a number to go straight to that question.

An annulled question is not shown, and the numbering jumps: for example, 48 comes after 46.

### Submit the exam
1. Choose **Submit exam**.
2. The confirmation says which questions are still blank. Choose **Cancel** to go back and answer, or confirm to submit.
3. The **Exam submitted** page shows the date and time of the submission, how many questions you answered and how many were left blank, and the **Back to the exam** button, which leads to the exam page.

After submitting, you cannot change your answers. The score and the key do not appear here yet; they come in a later version.

### Leave and continue later
Choose **Leave and continue later** in the top bar. Your answers are already saved; to continue, go back to the exam and choose **Take this practice exam** again.

## Messages
| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Your answer was not saved. The question is back as it was; mark it again. | The answer did not reach the server, for example because the connection dropped. | Mark the answer again. |
| This answer type is not in the simulator yet. The question stays blank and counts as blank. | The question is of a type the simulator does not take yet, such as the essay. | Nothing; skip the question. |
| The exam was not submitted. Your answers are still saved; try again. | The submission failed. | Choose **Submit exam** again. |
| The exam could not be loaded. | The simulator could not open the exam. | Reload the page. |
| The practice exam could not be started. Try again. | The practice exam could not be created. | Choose **Take this practice exam** again. |
| Page not found | The address is of a practice exam that is not yours or does not exist. | Open your practice exams from the catalog. |
