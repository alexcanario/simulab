# Getting around

Every page of Simulab has the same top bar and side menu, on a computer and on a phone.

## Who can use it
Everyone, signed in or not.

## How to
### Open a page from the menu
1. Choose an item in the side menu. The page you are on is highlighted.
2. On a phone or a narrow window, the menu is hidden: press the menu button (three lines, top left) to open it. It closes after you choose an item, when you press Esc or when you tap outside it.

### Make the menu smaller
1. On a computer, press the menu button to show only icons. Point at an icon to see its name.
2. Press it again to show the full menu. Simulab remembers your choice on this browser.

### Switch between light and dark mode
1. Press the moon icon in the top bar for dark mode, or the sun icon for light mode.
2. Simulab remembers your choice on this browser. Until you choose, it follows your device's setting.

### Change the language
1. Press the globe icon in the top bar and choose a language. The page reloads in that language and keeps your light or dark mode. When you are signed in, it also becomes your preferred language ([My account](my-account.md)).

### Use the keyboard
1. Press Tab once after a page loads: a "Skip to main content" link appears at the top left. Press Enter to jump straight to the page content.
2. Tab moves through the menu button, the Simulab name (back to the home page), the mode and language switches, and the menu items.
