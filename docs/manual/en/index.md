# Simulab

Simulab is a study coach. You practice with exams that feel like the real ones, see how you are doing, and find out what to study next.

## Who can use it
- **Students** practice, follow their progress and get study guidance.
- **Curators** import past exams, review them, write questions and publish content.
- **Admins** manage the catalog, users, roles and plans.

What you can use depends on your plan. Some features, such as the AI coach, may be available for a limited time with a promotional code.

## What you can do
Pages are added here as each feature is released.

| Area | What it is for | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Exam Simulator | Take a real past exam with the same questions, order, time limit and scoring rules | Coming soon |
| Question Bank Simulator | Build your own practice by subject, organizer, year or difficulty | Coming soon |
| Performance | See your results over time, by subject and topic, and how far you are from the cut-off score | Coming soon |
| Study recommendations | See which topics to study next | Coming soon |
| AI coach | Ask why an answer is right or wrong and get a study plan for your target exam | Coming soon |
| Account | Sign up, sign in, change your language and manage your data | Coming soon |

## Languages
Simulab is available in Portuguese (Brazil), Portuguese (Portugal) and English. You choose the language in your profile. Exam questions are always shown in their original language.
