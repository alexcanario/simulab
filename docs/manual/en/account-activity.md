# Recent activity

Shows your own recent security events — when you signed in, a failed sign-in, a password change, two-factor turned on or off — so you can tell your own actions from someone else's. If **My account → Security** has no **Recent activity** card, it is not available yet.

## Who can use it
Anyone signed in, for their own account only.

## How to
### See your recent activity
1. Open **My account** and select **Security**.
2. The **Recent activity** card, at the top of the page, lists your last 20 events, newest first: when, what happened, how or why (when it applies), and the address it came from (when known).

## Fields
| Field | Meaning | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| When | Date and time of the event | Shown in your time zone |
| Event | What happened | Same wording as the Account events page (Admins) |
| Details | How a sign-in was passed, or why it failed | Empty when neither applies |
| Address | Where the request came from | Empty when it was never known |

## Messages
| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| No activity recorded yet. | Nothing has happened to your account since it exists. | Nothing; events appear here as they happen. |
| We could not load your recent activity. | The page could not reach the server. | Select **Try again** |
