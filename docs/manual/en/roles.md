# Roles and permissions

Every account has a role that decides what it can see and do in Simulab.

## Who can use it
Everyone has a role. Only Admins can open the Roles page.

## How to
### Know your role
1. Every new account starts as a **Student**: you can practice and follow your progress.
2. **Curators** import and publish content; **Admins** manage the catalog, accounts, roles and plans.
3. There is no self-service way to become a Curator or an Admin yet: ask the team to change it for you.

### Open the Roles page (Admins only)
1. Sign in with an Admin account.
2. Choose **Roles**, in the **Administration** section of the side menu.
3. The page lists the roles that exist in Simulab today (shown by their internal names, Student, Curator, Admin, until F-9 adds a translated back office). Assigning them to an account is not available yet.

## Messages
| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Page not found | You opened a page your account's role does not give you access to. | Ask an Admin if you believe you should have access. |
