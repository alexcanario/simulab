# Create an account

Create your Simulab account with your email address, then confirm the address from the message we send you.

## Who can use it
Anyone aged 18 or older. You do not need an account to read the terms of use or the privacy policy.

## How to
### Create your account
1. Open **Create your account**.
2. Enter your email and a password, and type the password again to confirm it. Your full name is optional.
3. Tick the three boxes: that you are 18 or older, that you accept the terms of use, and that you agree with the privacy policy. Each document opens in a new tab.
4. Select **Create account**. The next page tells you where we sent the link.

### Confirm your email
1. Open the message **Confirm your email — Simulab** in your inbox.
2. Select **Confirm email**. If the button does not work, copy the address shown below it into your browser.
3. The page says **Email verified**: your account is active. Opening the same link again is not a problem.

### Ask for a new link
- On **Check your email**, wait for the counter to end and select **Send a new link**. Each new link replaces the previous one.
- If your link expired, the page that opens offers the same option: enter your email and ask for a new one.

## Fields
| Field | Meaning | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Email | The address that identifies your account | Required. A valid address, up to 254 characters |
| Password | The password for your account | Required. At least 12 characters, with an uppercase letter, a digit and a symbol. The bar below shows how close you are |
| Confirm password | The same password again | Required. Must be identical |
| Full name | How we address you | Optional, up to 120 characters |
| 18 or older | Your own declaration | Required |
| Terms of use / Privacy policy | Your acceptance of the current version of each document | Required |

## Messages
| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Draft text: the legal documents are under review and may still change. | The legal texts are not final yet | Nothing; read them before accepting |
| The legal texts changed while you were filling the form. Read them again and accept. | A new version was published while the page was open | Read the documents and tick the two boxes again |
| This password does not follow the rules above. | The password misses one of the rules | Choose a longer password with an uppercase letter, a digit and a symbol |
| Too many attempts from this device. Try again in an hour. | Many accounts were created from the same device | Wait an hour |
| This link expired | Links are valid for 24 hours | Ask for a new link on the same page |
| This link is not valid | The link was replaced by a newer one, or copied only in part | Use the newest message, or sign up again |

If the address you enter already has an account, you see the same page as a new sign-up and no new message is sent. This protects who is registered.
