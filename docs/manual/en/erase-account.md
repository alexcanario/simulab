# Erase your account

Erase your Simulab account yourself, whenever you want. Your personal data is removed straight away and cannot be brought back.

## Who can use it
Anyone who is signed in, for their own account only. Nobody can erase someone else's account.

## How to
### Erase your account
1. Open **My account** from the account menu in the top-right corner.
2. Scroll to **Erase my account**, at the bottom of the page.
3. Read what is erased and what is kept, then select **Erase my account**.
4. Type your current password and select **Erase my account** again.
5. Simulab signs you out on every device and takes you to the sign-in page with a farewell message.

You can stop at any point before step 4 by selecting **Cancel**.

### What is erased
- Your name, your email address and your password.
- Your sign-in sessions, on this and on every other device.
- The links Simulab sent you by email (confirmation and password reset).
- The roles your account held.

### What is kept
- Records that no longer say who you are, used for the statistics of the questions.
- The proof that you once accepted the Terms of use and the Privacy policy — the version, the date and the language, without your address. The law requires Simulab to keep it.

### After erasing
- Your email address is free again. You can create a new account with it, and it starts empty: the new account has nothing from the old one.
- Signing in with the old address and password no longer works, and asking for a password reset for it sends nothing.
- You receive one last email at that address saying what was erased. It is the last message Simulab sends you.

## Fields
| Field | Meaning | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Current password | Proof that the account is yours | Required; the same password you sign in with |

## Messages
| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The current password is not correct. | The password typed does not match your account; nothing was erased | Type it again. Too many wrong tries lock the account for a while |
| Too many attempts. Try again in mm:ss. | The account is locked after repeated wrong passwords | Wait for the time shown and try again |
| You are the last user able to manage roles. | Erasing your account would leave nobody able to manage roles in Simulab | Ask another Admin to take that permission first, then erase your account |
| Your account was erased. Thank you for the time you spent with us. | The erasure finished and you are signed out | Nothing. You can create a new account with the same address whenever you want |
